# From the CGB curve to a daily signal: evidence, backtest, and deployment

> Learning path · 90–120 minutes · Complete one ETF time-series Factor analysis and one backtest first.

This exercise connects the full operating chain: **derive a Factor from point-in-time China government bond yields, test its
historical relationship with future bond-ETF returns, use the same frozen Factor in a strategy backtest, then deploy it and audit
the Factor input and planned order for a specified signal date.**

The case uses the 5-year government bond ETF `511010.SH` and the built-in Factor `cgb_yield_decline_20`. A positive score means
the available 10-year government yield is below its level 20 trading observations earlier. That is consistent with the intuition
that falling yields may accompany rising bond prices, but intuition, a functioning system, and admissible strategy evidence are
three different things.

## The fixed case has been run for real

On 2026-08-25, a fresh account ran a hypothesis explore report, one formal holdout, a costed strategy backtest, deployment, and a
2026-07-30 daily signal. The run used the real product API, job queue, and browser UI. The deployment was paused and the strategy
was removed after acceptance; no broker was connected and no real trade was made.

| Item | Frozen setting |
| --- | --- |
| Factor | `cgb_yield_decline_20`, the 20-day decline in the 10Y CGB yield |
| Research assets | `511010.SH`, `511260.SH`, and `511090.SH` |
| Target | Each ETF's next-20-trading-day adjusted total return |
| Explore window | 2018-01-01 to 2025-01-27 |
| Formal holdout | 2025-02-05 to 2026-07-30 |
| Primary criterion | Median Newey–West t across the three ETFs `> 1.96` |
| Strategy rule | Hold 80% in `511010.SH` when its score is positive; otherwise hold cash |
| Backtest | CNY 1 million; 2bp slippage; impact coefficient 0.1; through 2026-07-30 |
| Signal / planned execution | 2026-07-30 / 2026-07-31 |

### Actual explore and holdout results

| Phase | ETF | Correlation | Newey–West t | Direction hit rate | Positive-state forward return | Negative-state forward return |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Explore | 5Y government bond ETF | 0.1779 | 2.044 | 59.05% | 0.38% | 0.17% |
| Explore | 10Y government bond ETF | 0.1443 | 1.951 | 59.20% | 0.48% | 0.24% |
| Explore | 30Y government bond ETF | 0.1418 | 1.356 | 59.83% | 1.54% | 1.09% |
| Holdout | 5Y government bond ETF | -0.3851 | -2.738 | 44.31% | 0.08% | 0.21% |
| Holdout | 10Y government bond ETF | -0.2972 | -1.918 | 45.77% | 0.05% | 0.22% |
| Holdout | 30Y government bond ETF | -0.2298 | -1.337 | 43.15% | -0.52% | -0.03% |

The explore phase contained 3,749 valid observations. Its median t was **1.951**, already just below the preregistered `> 1.96`
criterion. The formal holdout contained 1,029 observations; its median t reversed to **-1.918**, and every ETF's direction hit
rate fell below 50%. The research criterion therefore failed, with out-of-sample direction directly conflicting with the hypothesis.

### Actual backtest and daily signal

Successful use of the same frozen Factor by the strategy engine does not make the strategy effective. The fixed rule produced
-0.41% annualized return, -3.36% cumulative return, -7.67% maximum drawdown, -0.16 Sharpe, 7.68x turnover, and 183 trades.
Fees were CNY 31,139.73 and slippage loss was CNY 110,790.23.

![Actual costed backtest of the CGB-curve Factor strategy](/docs/images/help/zh/learning/cgb-signal-backtest-result.png)

On 2026-07-30, the frozen data cutoff was also 2026-07-30 and the decision score for `511010.SH` was 1.1900. The rule therefore
generated a planned buy of 5,400 shares at a CNY 140.85 reference price for the next trading day, 2026-07-31. This was still a
simulated instruction. “Pending settlement” and “pending actual fill” explicitly show that no broker fill was recorded.

![Factor input and planned order generated by the CGB-curve strategy](/docs/images/help/zh/learning/cgb-signal-daily-result.png)

The frozen verdict is: **the operating chain passed, but the research and strategy evidence failed; this should not remain enabled
as a production candidate.** The failure is retained to show that deployment capability cannot replace an evidence gate.

## What you will be able to do

You should be able to:

1. separate a Factor evidence report, strategy backtest, deployment snapshot, and daily signal;
2. audit the government-curve source, publication time, next-trading-day availability rule, and bp unit;
3. trace Factor key, code hash, inputs, and data cutoff from report to backtest to signal;
4. handle the `queued → running → done` job lifecycle instead of reporting queued work as failed; and
5. stop production deployment when the system runs correctly but the research gate or costed result fails.

## Step one: separate the four evidence layers

| Layer | Question answered | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Factor report | Is the historical score related to future returns? | Real trading performance |
| Strategy backtest | What historical path follows from explicit positions and costs? | Future persistence |
| Deployment snapshot | Which code and Factor dependencies will run each day? | That today will produce an order |
| Daily signal | Which data was read and which action was planned for one date? | That a broker filled the order |

Even after Factor research fails, the next three layers may work correctly as software. This exercise runs them for acceptance, but
that cannot change the final evidence verdict.

## Step two: freeze the curve hypothesis

Choose “CGB 10Y yield decline (20d)” from the Factor Library. Its definition is:

$$
S_t=100\times(y^{10Y}_{t-20}-y^{10Y}_t)
$$

The raw yield unit is percent, so `S` is measured in basis points. `S_t>0` means the yield fell. The product uses the official
Ministry of Finance China government yield curve and permits a curve observation only from the next SSE trading day after its
publication. A strategy cannot read the value before it was available.

In More settings, freeze the three government bond ETFs, a 20-trading-day forward return, 2018-01-01 through the current explore
cutoff, and automatic Newey–West lag. Enter this preregistration in the research card:

```text
Hypothesis: when the available China government 10Y yield has fallen over the previous
            20 trading observations, government bond ETFs tend to earn higher returns
            over the next 20 trading days.

Rationale: falling yields often accompany higher bond prices, but ETF duration, coupon carry,
           curve reshaping, costs, and publication timing may weaken the relationship.

Expected direction: positive.
Primary criterion: median Newey–West t across the three ETFs > 1.96.

Falsification: the formal holdout misses the criterion, signs reverse, hit rates fall below
               50%, or a costed strategy does not retain an acceptable result.
```

The `1.96` line is preregistered for this exercise; it is not a universal certification threshold. Do not lower it to `1.95` after
observing `1.951`.

## Step three: read explore evidence before opening the Lab

Audit the report in this order:

1. the only input is `rates.cgb.yield.10y`, with a 21-observation window;
2. the research assets are exactly the three government bond ETFs;
3. the data cutoff equals the explore end date;
4. each asset has valid coverage, correlation, robust t, and direction hit rate;
5. positive- and negative-state forward returns are compared across all assets; and
6. the report says it is signal evidence, not a strategy backtest.

The two shorter-duration ETFs look stronger, but the 30Y ETF's t is only 1.356 and the cross-asset median misses the gate by 0.009.
That is a failure to pass, not “approximately passed.”

## Step four: reveal the formal holdout once

Start Validate holdout from the original explore report. First confirm that the completed result stays sealed and that job logs do
not leak metrics. Then make one irreversible reveal and record the window, code hash, first reveal time, and frozen criterion.

In this case, all three correlations and t statistics became negative. Candidate explanations include a changed rate regime, duration
mismatch between ETF and curve point, movements elsewhere on the curve, coupon carry, overlapping 20-day targets, or explore-period
chance. The only current conclusion is that the relationship did not persist. Do not mine the same holdout for a rescue window.

## Step five: backtest the frozen Factor

Use this TypeScript strategy to accept the downstream integration:

```ts
const bond = '511010.SH';

export default defineStrategy({
  name: 'Learning case: CGB curve daily signal',
  watch: [bond],
  factors: ['cgb_yield_decline_20'],
  onBar(ctx) {
    const score = ctx.factor('cgb_yield_decline_20', bond);
    if (score != null && score > 0) {
      ctx.setHoldings({ [bond]: 0.8 });
    } else {
      ctx.setHoldings({});
    }
  },
});
```

Expand “Factors used by this backtest” and verify:

- key `cgb_yield_decline_20`;
- input `rates.cgb.yield.10y`;
- frozen Factor ID and code hash;
- 183 actual simulated fills, rather than an empty equity curve; and
- non-zero fees and slippage.

Negative annualized return and Sharpe reinforce the holdout failure. Do not isolate the smaller drawdown and rebrand the run as a
success.

## Step six: deployment only verifies the operating contract

Continue only in a safe test environment. Deploy freezes strategy code and settings, Factor key, Factor ID, code hash, curve input,
deployment time, and status.

After running a specified signal date, audit its signal date, planned execution date, model equity, Factor valid/observed counts,
mean, and decision value. The 1.1900 score on 2026-07-30 only proves that the frozen definition produced a positive score on that
date. It cannot overturn the full holdout or backtest.

Also distinguish:

- **instruction**: what the model plans for the next trading day;
- **simulated fill**: the execution used by model accounting; and
- **actual execution**: a broker result entered by the user.

“Pending settlement / actual fill” prevents a plan from being represented as a fill. Pause the deployment after acceptance.

## Step seven: write the verdict

```text
Research verdict: do not admit to production candidacy.

Evidence: explore median t was 1.951, below the preregistered >1.96 line; formal holdout
reversed to -1.918 across all three ETFs; the fixed strategy returned -0.41% annualized
after costs with a -0.16 Sharpe.

Engineering verdict: Factor lineage, queue lifecycle, backtest, deployment, specified-date run,
and next-day instruction all completed with an auditable record.

Action: pause the test deployment. Do not treat the 2026-07-30 buy plan as investment advice.
A genuinely new mechanism requires a new explore question and future unseen data, not reuse of this holdout.
```
