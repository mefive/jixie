# Share and copy public assets

The **Public Library** lists strategies and published Factors that users deliberately make public. Public visibility lets another user inspect a card and copy an independent private version; it does not create collaborative editing.

## Browse and copy

1. Open **Public Library** in the top navigation.
2. Under **Public assets**, browse **Public strategies** and **Public Factors**.
3. Verify the name, asset type, and author. A Factor also shows its key.
4. Select **Copy to my workspace**.
5. A strategy opens in the Backtest workspace and a Factor opens in Factor Research. The copy is private by default.
6. Before editing, inspect the code, parameters, research scope, and any existing report. Public origin does not remove the need for validation.

Copying creates a new owner and independent ID. Later changes or privacy settings on the source do not rewrite an existing copy, and your edits do not change the source.

![Public assets and the copy action in Public Library](/docs/images/help/zh/library/share-copy-01.png)

## Share your asset

1. Open the **My shares** tab in Public Library.
2. Find the asset under **My strategies** or **My published Factors**.
3. Select **Share publicly**.
4. Return to Public assets and verify its card and author.

Only a strategy you own can be shared. A Factor must first complete the Factor Research publication flow; a draft does not appear under My published Factors. Sharing does not change strategy backtests or Factor reports.

## Make an asset private

Select **Make private** from My shares or your own public card. New users can no longer copy it from the public list. Independent copies already created by other users are not deleted.

## Check before sharing

- Remove personal information, keys, and any material that should not be public from code, names, and descriptions.
- Verify keys, fields, and research scope.
- Do not present historical returns as a promise; every user must validate a public asset independently.
- Public Library is not a live-trading marketplace and does not imply platform endorsement.

## Common questions

### Why is my custom Factor absent from My shares?

Only published Factors can be shared. Complete an approved report and publish it; editable drafts remain private.

### Can making it private revoke someone else's copy?

No. The copy became an independent private asset owned by that user when it was created.
