# Use Research curation

**Research curation** performs read-only analysis of existing research conversations and failure records. It identifies recurring research protocols, data requests, documentation improvements, and tool-improvement candidates. It does not edit code, synchronize vendor data, change a roadmap, or publish a feature.

## Start curation

1. Open **Research** in the top navigation.
2. Select **Curate research records** on the left.
3. Select **Start curation** in the drawer.
4. Wait for the summary of source evidence, candidates, and repeated items.

![Research curation candidate summary](/docs/images/help/zh/research/curator-01.png)

## Read a candidate

Each candidate can show:

- its type, such as a research-protocol candidate;
- whether it matches a current system capability;
- expected value, suggested next step, and estimated change surface;
- matched capability identifiers and verification notes;
- supporting evidence, repetition, and synthesis confidence.

**Matched system capability** means a related entry exists in the current catalog or read-only capability list. It does not mean the method is validated or that the product will change immediately.

## Submit feedback

Candidates require human judgment. Use the offered accept, skip, or verification feedback to record a disposition. Accepting a candidate only sends it into a later human process; it does not implement a feature on this page.

Candidate acceptance, repetition, and verification-error rates describe curation quality. Do not treat a percentage based on a very small sample as stable evidence.

## Privacy and action boundaries

- Curation reads only research records available to the current account.
- It does not publish private research to the Public Library.
- It does not execute document Cells or create FactorReports or backtests.
- A candidate summary does not replace its source evidence; return to the document and snapshot for decisions.
