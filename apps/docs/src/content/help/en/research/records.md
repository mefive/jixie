# Use research records

The **Research history** sidebar stores the plain-language question, explanation, and typed artifacts together. Legacy screener chats and saved queries are migrated here during upgrade; there are no parallel product entries.

- Select a record to restore its messages and result cards.
- Use the edit control beside the title to rename it.
- A Universe card runs its saved spec automatically and can be rerun manually.
- Deleting a record removes that Research conversation and its messages. It does not delete market data, factors, or strategies.

A conversation records the working process. A later reproducibility milestone will additionally freeze protocol, parameters, data summary, environment fingerprint, and run-to-run differences.
