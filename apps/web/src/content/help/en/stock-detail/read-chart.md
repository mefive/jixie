# Read Stock detail and candlesticks

Stock detail places price candlesticks, PE, and volume in one chart. Use it to inspect historical price and valuation, not to conclude that a stock must rise or fall.

## Open Stock detail

1. Run a criteria screen.
2. Find a stock in the result table.
3. Select its row.
4. Stock detail opens in a new tab; the screen remains in the original tab.

## Page areas

![Stock name, adjustment, scale, and market chart](/help/zh/stock-detail/chart-overview-01.png)

The marks identify:

1. **Name and code**: confirm the intended stock.
2. **Adjustment**: forward-adjusted, after-adjusted, or unadjusted.
3. **Price scale**: linear or logarithmic.
4. **Chart**: candlesticks and PE above, volume below, and the time-range slider at the bottom.

## Read candlesticks

Each candlestick represents one trading day and includes open, close, high, and low prices.

- Red means close is at or above open.
- Green means close is below open.
- Price uses the left axis.
- Move the pointer over the chart to inspect values for a date.

Do not infer a trend from one candlestick. Select an appropriate range and inspect multiple trading days.

## Change the displayed period

Use the slider at the bottom:

1. Drag either end to shorten or expand the period.
2. Drag the middle to move the selected period.
3. Check the start and end dates on the horizontal axis.

Changing the visible period does not change the source data.

## Check the data range first

The rightmost date is the latest trading date available on the page. Check it before interpreting the chart, especially on non-trading days or before an update completes.

A future date not shown is not a zero price and should not be filled in manually.
